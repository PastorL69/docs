Did you know?

PPUC/DMD grew out of ZeDMD, the popular high-speed DMD for virtual pinball machines. In late 2024, with the full permission of its creator David "Zedrummer" Lafarge, the ZeDMD project was handed over to Markus Kalkbrenner, who turned it into a versatile solution that serves as a key building block of PPUC/DMD.
