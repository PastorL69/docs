How it works

From monochrome to full colour

  1. 01

    Read

    PPUC/DMD taps the original display signal of your machine. No changes to the game ROM.

  2. 02

    Colorize

    Every frame is matched against a Serum v1 or v2 colorization and coloured in real time.

  3. 03

    Display

    A ZeDMD drives bright RGB LED panels that replace the original dot matrix display.

See it in action

Did you know?

PPUC/DMD grew out of ZeDMD, the popular high-speed DMD for virtual pinball machines. In late 2024 the ZeDMD project was handed over to Markus Kalkbrenner, who turned it into a versatile solution, and serving as a key building block of PPUC/DMD.
